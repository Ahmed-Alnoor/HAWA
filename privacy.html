Privacy Policy

Website chat and WhatsApp assistant. Effective 5 November 2026.

This policy explains how Al Marwan Developments, the real estate arm of Al Marwan Group in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"), handles your information when you chat with our virtual sales assistant on our websites or on WhatsApp.

What we collect

  • The messages you send to the assistant.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, mobile number, the project or unit type you are interested in, your budget and the best time to contact you.
  • On WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number and profile name, which WhatsApp shares with us when you message us.
  • On our websites: a random chat session ID stored in your browser and the page you are chatting from.

Please do not share payment card details, passwords or identity documents in the chat.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions about our projects and send you photos, floor plans and locations.
  • To arrange a call back, a viewing or a visit to our sales office when you ask for one.
  • To check and improve the quality and accuracy of our replies.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who processes it for us

We use service providers to run the assistant: Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) to send and receive WhatsApp messages, n8n to run our automation, and OpenAI to generate the assistant's replies. They process your information on our behalf. Our sales team sees the details needed to follow up on your enquiry.

How long we keep it

Chat records are kept in our systems for a limited period for quality and security. Enquiry details you share with our sales team are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, or longer where the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. On WhatsApp you can also block our number at any time.
